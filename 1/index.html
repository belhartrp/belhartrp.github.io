01 / ABOUT

“I care about how things work just as much as how they look. And when I step away from the screen, I draw.”

I'm Belhart, a Computer Science student with a focus on programming, AI engineering, and digital design. I started with code, and somewhere along the way I realized that building something truly complete means caring about every layer of it — how it thinks, how it runs, and how it feels to use. That same thinking carries into my design work, and even into drawing. I don't see them as separate skills. To me, they are all just different ways of solving the same problem.
